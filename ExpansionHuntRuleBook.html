Hunt the Expansion

A Hunger Games Acronym Challenge

Six districts. Hidden words. One winner. And someone in your midst is already planning to betray you.

The Premise

What Is This Game?

An acronym is revealed. Its full form — split into individual word cards — is hidden around the auditorium. Districts race to find every word, bring cards back to their base, arrange them in the correct order, and present to the MC first. But players can pocket cards, raid rival bases, and secretly defect.

Quick Flow

How a Round Works

1
MC reveals the acronym. A 60-second strategy huddle — districts plan who hunts, who guards, who spies.
2
Whistle blows. Players scatter to find word cards hidden around the auditorium. Each word of the full form is a separate card.
3
Cards go to your district base (a marked table). Bases can be raided — unguarded cards can be taken by anyone.
4
When your district believes they have all words, arrange cards in order and call the MC over.
5
MC judges. First correct district wins the round. Points awarded. Traitors can be accused.
The Law

Core Rules

🗺 Hiding Cards

MC team hides all word cards before each round — under chairs, on windowsills, taped to walls. All cards stay within the auditorium boundary.

🏠 Base Camps

Each district has a marked table as home base. Collected cards must be placed on the table — you cannot hold all cards in your hands indefinitely.

🚫 No Snatching

You cannot grab a card from another player's hand. You may only take cards from an unguarded base table. No physical contact allowed.

⏱ Time Limit

Each round has a 5-minute time limit. If no district answers correctly in time, only 2nd and 3rd place points are awarded.

📋 Presenting

Lay all word cards on your base in correct order and shout your district name. The MC walks over to judge. Do not rush the MC.

❌ Wrong Answer

Incorrect arrangement = 30-second lockout before you can attempt again. Other districts may present during that window.

Conduct

Player Conduct

  • ▸No destroying, hiding, or discarding cards outside the play area
  • ▸No physically blocking other players
  • ▸Verbal bluffing, misdirection, and trash talk are fully allowed
  • ▸MC's decision is final on all disputes
  • ▸A player ejected for misconduct costs their district 2 points
The Dark Layer

Betrayal System

Anyone Can Betray. The Question Is Whether You Get Away With It.

Loyalty is optional. Points are personal.

HOW TO BETRAY

Pick up a word card from your own district's base. Instead of guarding it, walk it to another district's base and place it there quietly. No announcement required. The art is doing it without your own teammates noticing.

IF YOU'RE NOT CAUGHT

After the round, if no teammate formally accused you, earn +2 personal points. These appear on a separate individual leaderboard.

IF YOU'RE CAUGHT

A teammate must accuse you to the MC during or immediately after the round. If confirmed, you sit out the next round.

⚠ FALSE ACCUSATION

Accuse a teammate wrongly and your district loses 1 point. Don't throw accusations blindly.

🕵 THE SECRET SPY ROLE

Before the game, the MC privately assigns one Secret Spy per district — someone encouraged to betray at least once. A successful uncaught betrayal earns them +3 personal points instead of +2. All spy identities are revealed dramatically at the very end.

"May the odds be ever in your favour — and may you trust your district a little less than you trust yourself."

🃏 THE DECOY CARD

In rounds 7–10, one fake word card is hidden among the real ones — a plausible-sounding word that doesn't belong in the full form. Districts that include the decoy in their arrangement are wrong and face the 30-second lockout. Part of the challenge is knowing the actual full form.

Final Tally

🏆 DISTRICT CHAMPION

Most points after 10 rounds wins. On a tie, the tied districts face a sudden-death bonus round with a surprise acronym — first correct wins.

🗡 CAPITOL'S FAVOURITE

The individual player with the most personal betrayal points gets a separate award. Reveal their identity last, dramatically.

For the MC

Game Run Sheet

📦 PREP CHECKLIST (BEFORE EVENT)

  • Print word cards for all 10 rounds on correct coloured paper. Cut each word into a separate card.
  • Assign one Secret Spy per district privately before players arrive. Slip them a note.
  • Mark 6 base tables around the room perimeter with district colour labels.
  • Set up live scoreboard (whiteboard or projected screen).
  • Hide Round 1 word cards before players enter the room.
  • Brief any assistants on the betrayal rules and what to watch for.
