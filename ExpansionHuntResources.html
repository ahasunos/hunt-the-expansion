For the MC

MC Master Checklist

📦 DAY-BEFORE PREP

  • Print all word cards on correct coloured paper (10 sets, one per round)
  • Cut every word into a separate card. Label round number on the back in pen
  • Print decoy cards for rounds 7–10 on matching paper. Mark back with "X" (MC only)
  • Print spy assignment cards (6 total). Keep them sealed in envelopes by district colour
  • Print scoreboard and individual tracker sheets (or set up projected version)
  • Gather: whistle, marker pen, timer, sticky tape for hiding cards

🌅 DAY-OF SETUP (BEFORE PLAYERS ARRIVE)

  • Mark 6 base tables around the room perimeter with district colour labels
  • Hide Round 1 word cards (NASA) around the auditorium — vary locations well
  • Decide privately which player in each district is the spy. Hand them their envelope
  • Brief any assistant MCs on betrayal rules and what to watch for
  • Test scoreboard visibility from all corners of the room

🎮 DURING THE GAME — PER ROUND

  • Announce acronym dramatically. Give 60 seconds for huddle — no movement.
  • Blow whistle. Start 5-minute timer visibly.
  • Walk to a district that calls for judging. Check card order against answer key.
  • Wrong? Announce it. Start their 30-second lockout clock.
  • Correct? Announce winner. Whistle. Game pauses.
  • Open 30-second betrayal accusation window. Resolve claims.
  • Update scoreboard. Hide next round's cards while players stay at bases.
  • Narrate tension: "District IV has 5 cards… District II is still searching…"

🏁 END OF GAME

  • Announce district scores dramatically, from 6th to 1st place
  • Reveal the individual betrayal leaderboard
  • Reveal all 6 spy identities one by one — pause for reactions
  • Award Capitol's Favourite to the top betrayer
  • If district tie: sudden-death bonus round with a new acronym
